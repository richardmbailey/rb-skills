Connecting…

The physical model builds an unrestricted layered pile and moves material through eight neighbours whenever the distance-corrected slope exceeds the selected repose angle. Model and angle changes apply on Reset. Source and jitter changes apply to the next batch without clearing the pile. Radius zero sends every grain to the exact middle site. The classic BTW mode remains available for comparison and is necessarily limited to heights from zero to three.

This finite simulation offers a visual and descriptive diagnostic. A straight-looking log–log plot, an estimated exponent, or a small KS distance does not by itself prove that the data follow a power law. The physical and BTW modes define avalanche size differently, so their observations must not be pooled. Formal distribution comparison and uncertainty analysis would be needed for a defensible statistical claim.
